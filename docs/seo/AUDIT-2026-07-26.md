# Wedding Wala — Live Free-Tool SEO Audit (2026-07-26)

Audited weddingwala.pk across multiple free tools (SiteAudit MCP, PageSpeed MCP,
Lighthouse MCP, Chrome UX Report / CrUX, seo-audit MCP). The SEMrush group-buy
project (campaign 30566806) was **not retrievable** — it's a shared seat and the
project has rotated out of the pool — so this audit is built from direct,
authoritative crawls of the live pages instead.

## Big correction to the earlier diagnosis
The numeric UX pages (`/photographers/2522`, `/venues/3273`) are **`noindex, follow`
BY DESIGN** — `app/(main)/(vendorListings)/layout.tsx` de-indexes them on purpose;
they parallel the real SEO routes. The pages that actually rank are the **3-level
canonical routes**, and they are **healthy**:

| Page | Score | H1 | Title | JSON-LD | Words |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `/wedding-photographers` (hub) | 94 | ✅ | unique | 4 blocks | 884 |
| `/wedding-photographers/karachi` (city) | 94 | ✅ | unique | 4 blocks | 1,575 |
| `/wedding-photographers/karachi/{name}-2522` (leaf) | 91 | ✅ | unique | 4 blocks | 746 |
| `/` (home) | 91 | ✅ | — | 2 blocks | 1,339 |

So the SSR/metadata/schema foundation is **solid**. The low keywords / ranking
slide are driven by the issues below — NOT by "client-rendered pages" as first thought.

## Verified issues (most impactful first)

### 1. Sitemap covers only ~670 URLs for a 4,000+ vendor site — HIGH
`sitemap.xml` exists, is referenced in robots, format valid — but **urlCount = 670**.
With 3,000+ vendors, the vast majority of vendor leaf pages are **missing from the
sitemap**, so Google is never told about them → under-indexation → low keyword
coverage. Root cause to fix in `app/sitemap.ts` (pagination/inclusion of the full
vendor set). **This is the single biggest indexation lever.**

### 2. Thin / duplicate content on ~3,268 "unclaimed" OSM pages — HIGH
Leaf pages for scraped listings carry boilerplate: *"Photographer listed from public
records in {city}. Unclaimed — full details to be added by the owner."* Thousands of
near-identical thin pages are a domain-wide quality drag (Panda-class) and a likely
cause of the ranking slide. Fix: **noindex until claimed/enriched** (keep followable),
and lean on the new vendor-fillable content to promote them back to index once real.

### 3. Core Web Vitals / server response — HIGH
- CrUX (field, real users): **LCP 5.57 s (POOR)**, TTFB **1,473 ms (poor)**, CLS 0 ✅, INP 141 ms ✅.
- SiteAudit: homepage **server response 1,974 ms** (aim <500 ms).
- Lighthouse mobile: perf 86 lab, but **LCP 3.4 s** — hero image + TTFB are the drag.
- Only **37% of images use `srcset`/`sizes`** (responsive) → oversized LCP images.

### 4. Broken social links (404) — MED
Footer + Organization JSON-LD `sameAs` point to dead handles:
`linkedin.com/company/weddingwalapk`, `youtube.com/@weddingwalapk`,
`twitter.com/weddingwalapk` all **404**. Bad E-E-A-T + broken-link signal. Fix:
correct or remove the dead profiles from the footer and the JSON-LD.

### 5. Title tags too long — MED
62–73 chars on home/leaf/city templates (SERP truncates ~60). Tighten the title
templates (drop the `| Wedding Wala` suffix on long titles, or shorten the pattern).

### 6. Meta descriptions off-length — LOW/MED
Home 174 (too long), leaf 102 (too short, and it's the "unclaimed" boilerplate).
Normalize to 150–160 and stop shipping the boilerplate as the description.

### 7. Images missing alt text — MED
Home 11/30 missing, hub 2/2, city 3/10, leaf 2/11. Systematic — add alt in the
templates (vendor name / "{type} in {city}" patterns).

### 8. Home `og:image` missing — MED
PageSpeed reports `ogImage: null` on `/` → poor social/link previews. Add a default OG image.

### 9. Security header: no CSP — LOW
HTTPS ✅, HSTS ✅, X-CTO ✅, X-Frame ✅, Referrer ✅, Permissions ✅. Missing only CSP.

### 10. Off-page: authority is very low — (not code)
SEMrush: Authority Score 8, 7 referring domains, 13 backlinks. Rankings won't hold
without links. Needs a real link-building/digital-PR push (separate workstream).

## Robots.txt — healthy ✅
AI crawlers (GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, Perplexity, Claude, Google-Extended, Bingbot,
CCBot, etc.) all allowlisted; only private surfaces disallowed; sitemap declared.

## Fix plan (code, single commit where possible)
1. **Sitemap**: include the FULL vendor inventory (fix pagination/inclusion) → 670 → ~all.
2. **Thin pages**: `noindex, follow` unclaimed/low-completeness leaves until enriched.
3. **Broken social links**: correct/remove dead handles (footer + Organization LD).
4. **Titles**: tighten templates under 60 chars.
5. **Meta descriptions**: 150–160; never ship the "unclaimed" boilerplate as description.
6. **Alt text + og:image + responsive images**: template-level.
7. Perf (LCP/TTFB) + CSP + backlinks: larger follow-ups (documented).
